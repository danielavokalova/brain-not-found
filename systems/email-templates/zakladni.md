# Basic Email Templates

## 1) Reply to client interest

### Version 1 (Friendly)

**Subject:** Thanks for your interest

Hi [Name],  

thanks for reaching out and for your interest in [service/product].  
I would be happy to look into it in more detail.

Could you please send me:

- what exactly you need,
- your ideal timeline,
- and your budget (if you have one).

Then I can suggest the next step.

Thanks a lot,  
[Your name]

### Version 2 (Formal)

**Subject:** Thank you for your interest

Hello [Name],  

thank you for your interest in [service/product].  
I would be glad to suggest the best next steps.

To prepare a specific proposal, please share:

- what exactly you need,
- your preferred timeline,
- and your budget (if applicable).

Once I have this information, I will follow up with a clear proposal.

Best regards,  
[Your name]

---

## 2) Follow-up reminder

### Version 1 (Friendly)

**Subject:** Quick follow-up

Hi [Name],  

just following up on my previous email about [topic].  
Is this still relevant for you?

If yes, just reply and I will suggest the next step.

Thanks and have a great day,  
[Your name]

### Version 2 (Formal)

**Subject:** Follow-up on my previous email

Hello [Name],  

I am following up on my previous email regarding [topic].  
Is this still relevant on your side?

If so, I would be happy to suggest the next step.

Thank you and have a nice day,  
[Your name]

---

## 3) Polite rejection

### Version 1 (Friendly)

**Subject:** Thanks for reaching out

Hi [Name],  

thanks for your message/offer.  
Unfortunately, I cannot take this on right now.

I do not want to promise something I cannot deliver with the right quality and timing.  
If you want, I can recommend someone else.

Thanks for understanding,  
[Your name]

### Version 2 (Formal)

**Subject:** Thank you for your offer

Hello [Name],  

thank you for your message and trust.  
After consideration, I am unable to confirm this collaboration at the moment.

I do not want to commit to a timeline I cannot reliably meet right now.  
If helpful, I can recommend an alternative.

Thank you for your understanding,  
[Your name]

---
